## Who Is This For?

These workshops are designed for you if you:

- [x] Lead or manage a team that builds or uses AI products
- [x] Are responsible for technology decisions without being a programmer
- [x] Want to have more confident conversations with engineers and data scientists
- [x] Need to communicate AI initiatives to stakeholders or boards
- [x] Want to build responsible, trustworthy AI products

!!! note "No technical background needed"

    Every concept is explained in plain language. Technical terms are always defined when they appear. You'll never be asked to write code.

---

## Before You Begin

Each workshop is 60–90 minutes and works as a standalone session. To get the most out of them:

1. **Read the overview** at the start of each workshop — it tells you exactly what you'll learn.
2. **Do the reflections** — short questions that help you connect the content to your own context.
3. **Try the actions** — each workshop ends with practical steps you can take immediately.
