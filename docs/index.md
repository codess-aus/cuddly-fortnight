## How These Workshops Work

Each workshop is a standalone session you can complete at your own pace or in a group. Here's what to expect:

| Feature | Details |
|---|---|
| ⏱ **Duration** | Each workshop takes 60–90 minutes |
| 🤝 **Style** | Hands-on activities with clear explanations |
| 📋 **Prerequisites** | Noted clearly at the start of each workshop |
| 💬 **Language** | Plain English — jargon is always explained |

---

## What You'll Explore

=== "Artificial Intelligence"

    - What AI actually is (and isn't)
    - How modern AI tools work
    - Real-world applications in business and everyday life
    - How to use AI tools responsibly

=== "GitHub"

    - What GitHub is and why it matters
    - Creating and managing repositories
    - Collaborating with others on projects
    - Using GitHub for your own work

=== "Product Development"

    - How ideas become products
    - Agile and iterative thinking
    - User research and feedback loops
    - Launching and improving products over time

## Why These Workshops Exist

Technology is changing every industry. AI tools are becoming part of everyday work — in schools, businesses, government, and creative fields. These workshops exist because everyone deserves to understand and participate in that change, not just professional developers.

> "You don't need to be able to write code to understand how AI works or to lead a team that builds with it."
