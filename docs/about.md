# About Workshop Hub

Workshop Hub is a free, open-source collection of workshops designed to make AI and technology education accessible to everyone — not just people with technical backgrounds.

---

## Our Purpose

Technology is changing how we work, learn, and create. But most of the education about that technology is aimed at people who already know a lot about it.

We built Workshop Hub because we believe:

- **Leaders** who manage technology teams deserve clear, jargon-free resources
- **Students** who are new to technology deserve a welcoming starting point
- **Everyone** benefits when more people understand AI — not just engineers

---

## What Makes These Workshops Different

=== "Accessible Language"

    Every technical concept is explained in plain language. Jargon is always defined. You should never need to search something up just to understand a sentence.

=== "Practical Focus"

    Each workshop includes activities, reflections, and actions you can take immediately. Learning is most valuable when you can apply it.

=== "Honest About Limitations"

    We tell you what AI can't do, where it fails, and what the genuine risks are. We don't oversell the technology.

=== "Free and Open"

    These workshops are free to use, share, and adapt. The source code is on GitHub — you can contribute improvements or create your own version.

## Our Values

| Value | What It Means in Practice |
|-------|--------------------------|
| **Clarity** | Plain language over jargon, always |
| **Honesty** | We acknowledge limitations, not just possibilities |
| **Inclusion** | Designed for people who are new to this, not experts |
| **Responsibility** | We take AI ethics seriously throughout |
| **Open Access** | Free for everyone, forever |
